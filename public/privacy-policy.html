Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to ChaloJi. This Privacy Policy describes how ChaloJi ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and handles your information when you use our Passenger and Driver applications. By using ChaloJi, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Information: Name, phone number, and profile photo — used for account creation, identification, and communication.
  • Location Data (Passengers): We collect foreground location to find nearby drivers and provide accurate pickup/drop routing.
  • Background Location Data (Drivers): We collect and use your precise device location even when the app is minimized or not in use. This is essential to assign nearby ride requests to you in real-time, display your live position to the passenger during the ride, and ensure passenger safety. This background location access is a core operating requirement of the ChaloJi Driver platform.
  • Camera & Photos: We request camera access so you can upload your profile picture and (for Drivers) submit mandatory vehicle and KYC document photos for verification.
  • Phone Number: Used exclusively for OTP-based login and ride-related communication. We do not share your number with third parties.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To match passengers with nearby verified drivers.
  • To send OTP verification, ride alerts, and important app notifications.
  • To monitor app usage, improve service quality, and ensure passenger safety.
  • To process payments and generate ride receipts.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to any third parties. Location data is shared between passengers and their assigned driver during an active ride only, solely for navigation purposes.

5. Data Security

We use Firebase (Google) infrastructure for secure data storage and authentication. While we implement industry-standard security measures, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Data Deletion

You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time. To submit a deletion request, please contact us at support@chalojii.in or use the delete option in the app settings.
